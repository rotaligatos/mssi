Privacy / Data Privacy Act of 2012

How we handle the information you give us.

Module System and Services, Inc. (“MSSI”, “we”) collects only what we need to quote, build, deliver and install your work, or to consider you for a job. This notice explains what we collect, why, who sees it, how long we keep it, and what you can ask of us under Republic Act No. 10173, the Data Privacy Act of 2012.

What we collect

Only what you type in or upload yourself:

  • Orders, service requests and site visit requests — your name, company, email, mobile number, site or delivery address, project details, target dates, the sales agent who referred you, how you heard about us, notes, your cutting list, and any drawings or photos you attach.
  • A client account, if you choose to sign in — your email address and the name shown on your Google account, plus the profile details you save so you do not have to type them again. If you invite colleagues to your company account, their email addresses.
  • Job applications — your name, contact details, city, work history, expected salary, start date, the role you applied for, and your CV.
  • Basic technical records — to stop automated abuse of the forms, the system notes how often requests arrive from the same connection for a short time. We do not use advertising trackers on this website.

Please do not send us sensitive personal information (for example health, government ID numbers or bank details) through these forms. We do not need it.

Why we use it

  • To prepare quotations, cutting and production, and to schedule delivery, installation and site visits.
  • To contact you about your order or request, and about aftercare on work we have done.
  • To keep a record of your past orders, so you can see and reorder them.
  • To understand which channels bring clients to us (from “how did you hear about us”).
  • For job applications, to evaluate you for employment.
  • To meet our legal obligations, such as keeping business and tax records.

We process this information because you asked us for a quotation, a service or a job, and because you consent to it when you send a form. We do not sell your information, and we do not use it to send you advertising from other companies.

Who can see it

  • Our staff who handle your order, request or application — estimating, production, logistics, installation, aftercare, and hiring for applications.
  • Our sister companies, World Class Laminate, Inc. and Cebu World Laminate, Inc., share the same order and production systems with us. Their staff may see your order where they supply materials or help fulfil it.
  • The service providers that run our systems for us — database hosting (Supabase), sign-in (Google), and website hosting. They hold the information on our behalf and may not use it for their own purposes.
  • Your own company account — if you invite colleagues, they can see the orders sent from that company account. Nobody else can join it without your invitation.

Your orders, attachments and CV are never published on this website.

Where it is kept, and how it is protected

Your information is stored in a secured database hosted in Singapore. Applications may also be copied to our own company file server in the Philippines. Access is limited by sign-in and by role: a client can only see their own orders and their invited company’s, and only authorised staff can see client records, attachments and applications. Files you upload are not public: they can only be opened by people allowed to see them.

How long we keep it

We keep order and project records for as long as we have a business relationship with you and afterwards for as long as the law requires us to keep business and tax records, and to support aftercare on work we have installed. Job applications that do not lead to employment are kept on file for future openings unless you ask us to delete them. When information is no longer needed, we delete it or make it anonymous.

Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act you may:

  • be informed about how your information is used (this notice);
  • ask for a copy of the information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct it if it is wrong or incomplete;
  • object to its use, or ask us to delete or block it, where we have no legal reason to keep it;
  • withdraw your consent at any time — this does not affect what was done before, but we may then be unable to complete an order still in progress;
  • receive your information in a common electronic format;
  • be compensated for damage caused by inaccurate, unlawfully obtained or unauthorised use of your information; and
  • file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).

To use any of these, write to us at the address below. We may need to confirm it is you before we act on the request.

Signing in

You do not need an account to send an order or request. If you sign in with Google, Google tells us your email address and name only — never your password, contacts or files. If you sign in with an emailed link, we use only the email address you give us.
