Site Visit Request

Book us to come and measure. Accurate measurements need a finished area — please read the condition below before booking.

Captured to CRM
Before you book — the area must be finished

We measure against the final surfaces. Please make sure the area is complete before the visit:

  • Floor tiles laid — and grouted
  • Ceiling installed — including cornices or drops
  • Walls plastered and painted — final coat

Why it matters: tile and plaster can add 10–20 mm per surface. Cabinetry cut to unfinished walls will not fit once the finishes go on — and that is a re-fabrication at your cost, not a site adjustment. If the area is not ready, book the visit for after the finishes are done.
